# ADR-WP1C — pure offline predictive package

Status: proposed for independent acceptance under Issue #11; offline scope approved.
Base: `2106166a4e60ffa8bc7d5870f46f98131f9084bf`. ADR01–07 remain binding.

## Decisions

1. Keep thermal, planning, learning and fan policy as immutable pure functions. Time, timezone, sources, limits, effective power and permissions are injected. No store, simulator, network, filesystem, timer or actuator imports. The previously accepted kernel/store/simulator are unchanged.
2. Preserve the pinned legacy hourly Euler room/stored equations and coefficient bounds. Add explicit interval ventilation-loss multiplier and external gain in °C/hour. Stored heat is a model state, not a measured floor surface or raw return probe. Unknown sources need supplied bounds and invalid quality; no inferred household coefficients, nominal measured energy or airflow.
3. Use a deterministic bounded beam (default width4, maximum16), at most864 contiguous slots of at most300s and72h. Rank current feasible candidates by needed morning continuity, integrated comfort deficit, continuity progress before deadline, soft known-spend budget overrun, unknown-price exposure, dated tariff cost and energy. This heuristic does not prove global optimality or physical infeasibility. `SEARCH_EXHAUSTED` and `BLOCKED` carry no heat segments. Beam expansion retains no more than255 ON runs to fit the accepted256-segment Plan contract; additional conditional splits that exceed capacity block rather than discard labels. These are compute/payload limits, not a global heating-session cap.
4. Morning advance applies only if a supplied valid scheduled/emergency outage intersects the morning. Bound the advance to180min; do not reinterpret B08. Confirmed elapsed credit must be contiguous to the injected current time, fit elapsed eligible morning time, and be explicitly qualified. Minimum duration and predicted energy/comfort are separate results. Safety may prevent the minimum.
5. Bind proposals to input hash, config/model/topology revisions, boot and owner/grid epochs. Bridge to an existing `Plan` only against the current complete stamp. A Plan remains a proposal to the WP1A reducer. Minor refresh retention replays all current thermal/window/grid constraints and then resolves the fixed mask; explicit material changes force optimization and record causes. Consumers must classify major source/equipment/strategy changes and advance revisions/hash; live classification/transport is not implemented here.
6. Keep initial, prestart, original12h and latest curves separate. Original selection requires issue<=reference−43200 and actual point coverage. No historical recomputation/backfill. Curves carry input hash, model revision, plan ID, source issue times and checksum. Optional absent provenance remains absent (model revision0/plan ID`missing`), never a verified import. This PR supplies immutable records, not a durable archive service.
7. Fit challenger coefficients locally with bounded ridge regression. Qualification checks7 independent training dates and3 holdout origin dates for every required horizon/regime, timestamp separation, source quality, 20% limits against parent and day baseline (zero remains zero), MAE and signed worst underprediction. Comparison tolerance1e−12°C only handles binary arithmetic at decimal thresholds. Approval, parent CAS and **recomputed prospective backtests matching the immutable receipt** are required for promotion; AI-supplied metrics/receipt alone cannot promote. Rollback advances model revision and does not touch energy/history. Atomic persistent model publication is deferred.
8. Fan arbitration returns demand descriptions. Unknown/stale sensor clears fireplace demand and continuity without claiming observed extinction; last confirmed fire state remains explicitly unqualified. Manual preserves ownership. STOP clears requests. Night900s uses a request edge, does not renew while held, and cannot stop remaining fireplace demand. Unknown trend cannot justify intense speed. All speeds are explicit inputs.
9. WP1C wire schema1.0 is strict, duplicate-key/nonfinite/unknown-field/type rejecting, bounded512KiB. A full864-slot72h request is slightly over256KiB with explicit forcing fields;512KiB permits the declared horizon. Prior WP1A schema and size limits are unchanged. Payload validation/serialization has no persistence authority.

## Consequences and gates

The offline algorithms are independently testable without HA or household telemetry. Coefficient, weather, duty, stage and power assumptions remain synthetic. Supplied qualified input is a test premise, not a household credential or physical measurement proof. A future separately authorized worker must provide revision fencing, trustworthy source qualification, archive persistence, deadlines, durable due-slot receipts and atomic champion publication. No live scheduler, adapter, UI, gateway or WP2A is included. G1/B01–B08 and target/physical gates remain BLOCKED.
